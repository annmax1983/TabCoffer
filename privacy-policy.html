TabCoffer Privacy Policy

TabCoffer keeps your blocklist and settings in your browser. Site blocking runs locally; the only data that can leave your device is what your browser's own account sync copies, plus the optional Pro licence check. No analytics, no account, no advertising.

01Data Collection

TabCoffer does not collect your browsing data:

  • Browsing history or URLs visited
  • Page text, images, or content
  • Cookies or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or telemetry

The only data that can reach our server is what the optional licence check sends, and only after you activate a licence: a random installation identifier, browser name and version, interface language, timezone, user agent, and the request IP address (kept for rate limiting only). Section 04 describes this in full.

02Permissions

TabCoffer requests minimal permissions:

  • declarativeNetRequest — Blocks websites using declarative rules. Does not read page content.
  • storage — Saves your blocklist, timers and settings in your browser. If account sync is on, your browser may copy them to your other devices; nothing is sent to TabCoffer servers.
  • alarms — Manages temporary bypass timers. No background tracking.
  • activeTab — Only accesses the current tab when you interact with the extension.
  • Content script on twelve supported sites — Runs only on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit, Snapchat, VK, Pinterest, Threads, LinkedIn, X and Xiaohongshu, and only while Surgical Blocking is switched on. It locates the site’s own short-video shelf or recommendation feed and adds one CSS class to hide it. It does not read, store, copy or transmit page content, and it sends nothing anywhere.
  • Host access to api.annmax1983.com — Used only to activate and validate an optional licence.
The <all_urls> host permission is required because you choose the blocked sites at runtime, so they cannot be listed in the package in advance. It is used to install a declarativeNetRequest redirect rule for the domains you add, and for nothing else. TabCoffer only acts on sites you explicitly block, or on feeds you explicitly hide.

03Data Storage & Browser Sync

Your blocklist and settings are stored with storage.sync, the browser's own account sync. If you are signed in, Google, Microsoft or Mozilla may copy them to your other computers. The developer operates no sync server and cannot read this data. Uninstalling removes the local copy; a copy already in your browser account is controlled by the browser vendor and can be removed in the browser's sync settings. The optional password is stored the same way, as a salted SHA-256 hash — never the plaintext.

04Third-Party Services

Core blocking features make zero network requests. Data can leave your device in two cases. If you enable the browser's own account sync, Google, Microsoft or Mozilla may copy your blocklist and settings to your other computers; the developer operates no sync server and cannot read that copy. If you voluntarily activate a paid VKT Pro licence, a random install identifier and browser metadata are sent to api.annmax1983.com to activate and validate the licence. The licence request never includes your blocklist, browsing history or personal data. Our licence server records the IP address of these requests solely for rate limiting and abuse prevention — never for profiling, advertising, or sharing with third parties.

05No ads or tracking

TabCoffer has no ads, no advertising trackers, no analytics and no hidden data collection.

06Data retention & deletion

Your settings and content stay in your browser until you delete them in the extension or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes the extension's local data. Nothing is retained on our servers, because your content is never sent to them. If you enabled a feature that uses your browser account's sync, a copy may remain in that account. You can manage or remove it in your browser's sync settings.

07Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will be noted in the extension's release notes.
